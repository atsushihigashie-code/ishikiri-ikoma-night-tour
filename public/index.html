One Coin Self-Guided Tour

Sacred Sites & Skyline Views

An easy, self-guided night out — free skyline views, a historic mountain cable car, and a quiet shrine town, all reachable by ordinary train. No hiking, no tickets to book in advance. ¥500, year-round.

How it works

Buy once, then follow the route at your own pace — each stop unlocks a short audio guide, background, and practical directions to the next. Meet at Aramoto Station around 5pm (autumn season).

Tonight's stops

  • Aramoto Station — meeting point
  • Higashiosaka City Hall — a free 22F skyline opener
  • Ikoma Cable — a historic cable railway up the mountain
  • Ikomasanjo Star Plaza — an award-winning night view deck
  • Dinner break near Ikoma Station
  • The Approach Street — fortune tellers, shopfronts, and a rare chance to talk with locals
  • Ishikiri Tsurugiya Shrine — a healing shrine with centuries of tradition, tour finale
Note: the mountaintop park's night hours vary by season. You can check its operating calendar here (Japanese — your phone's browser can usually translate it) before you go. If it's closed tonight, you'll choose an alternate route after unlocking the tour — a scenic cable ride to Hozanji, a Buddhist temple partway up the mountain, before continuing on to the same shrine finale. You won't miss out on the finale.
Before you buy: you'll need Google Maps to get between stops. The app (iOS / Android) or the browser version at maps.google.com both work fine — no need to install anything if you'd rather use your phone's browser.
