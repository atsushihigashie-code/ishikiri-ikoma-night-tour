Two scenic routes to the same shrine finale — pick whichever suits tonight.

Summit route · 9 stops

Visit Ikomasanjo Summit Deck

Aramoto Station → Higashiosaka City Hall → Ikoma Cable → Star Plaza summit deck → dinner → approach street → Ishikiri Tsurugiya Shrine. Available when the mountaintop park is running its night hours.

 Hozanji route · 9 stops

Visit Hozanji Temple

Aramoto Station → Higashiosaka City Hall → Hozanji Line cable car → Hozanji Temple → dinner near Ikoma → approach street → Ishikiri Tsurugiya Shrine. A scenic ride up to a historic mountainside temple, followed by the same shrine finale.
Not sure which to pick? Ikoma Cable only runs departures after 18:00 on days the mountaintop park operates night hours — that's when the Summit route is available. Check the current cable car timetable if you'd like the summit deck; otherwise, the Hozanji route runs year-round.
