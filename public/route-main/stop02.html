Ishikiri Tsurugiya Shrine

石切劔箭神社 · "Ishikiri-san"

Tradition holds this shrine was founded in the second year of Emperor Jinmu's reign. Locals know it as Denbo no Kamisama — a place associated with healing, especially freedom from illness. The grounds are open 24 hours.

Walk the approach street lined with fortune-telling shops — most are Japanese-only, so a translation app helps if you want to try one; think of it as an optional side-quest rather than the main event.
