Getting here: take an Express, Semi-Express, Sub-Express, or Local train on the Kintetsu Nara Line — the Rapid Express does not stop at Ishikiri. If you're coming by subway instead, Shin-Ishikiri Station (Chuo Line / Kintetsu Keihanna Line) is closer to the shrine, but note it sits on a different fare zone, so an Osaka Metro day pass alone won't cover it.
